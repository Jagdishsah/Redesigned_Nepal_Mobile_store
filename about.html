About Nepal Mobile Store

Pioneering transparent mobile commerce and tech choices in Nepal.

Welcome to Nepal Mobile Store — your trusted destination for genuine smartphones, flagship releases, and affordable mobile choices across Nepal. Originally created as a Grade 12 Computer Science project at Prasadi Academy, this platform has evolved into a modern, full-featured web storefront.

Our Mission

To empower smartphone buyers in Nepal with accurate market prices in Nepalese Rupees (NPR), transparent specifications, and a seamless shopping experience.

Our Vision

To establish Nepal's most reliable and user-centric mobile catalog, bridging official manufacturers (Apple, Samsung, Xiaomi, OnePlus, Nothing, Nokia) directly to tech enthusiasts across the country.

Why Choose Us?

Real-Time NPR Prices

Market prices updated from top Nepali tech portals like GadgetByte Nepal.
